About

GTA 6 Clipper is a personal, self-hosted tool. It watches public live streams of Grand Theft Auto VI, finds the funniest and most exciting moments, cuts them into short vertical clips, and posts them to the YouTube and TikTok accounts of the person who runs this server. Only clips of streamers who allowed it are posted.

Nobody else signs in to this app; it acts only on its owner's own accounts.
