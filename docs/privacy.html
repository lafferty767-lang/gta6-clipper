Privacy policy

This app is used only by the owner of this server, on their own accounts.

What it accesses. When the owner connects YouTube or TikTok, the app receives sign-in tokens for that one account. It uses them only to upload clips the owner made with this app and to show which channel is connected. It does not read your videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

Where it's kept. Tokens and clips are stored only on this server. They are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the platform they belong to (Google/YouTube or TikTok) to upload the owner's clips.

Live stream data. Public chat messages from the streams being watched are counted to detect exciting moments and are not stored.

Google data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are provided by the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access. Disconnect the app any time at Google account permissions or in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps. Deleting this server deletes everything it stored.
